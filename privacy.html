Privacy Policy

How this app handles your data

This policy explains what the automation app accesses when you connect a Facebook Page, what it does with that access, and how to revoke it at any time.

App: Auto Bot Operator: Ouk Channlyhour Effective: 26 July 2026

Auto Bot (“the App”) is a personal content & marketing tool operated by Ouk Channlyhour (“we”, “us”). It schedules and publishes posts, replies to comments and messages, and manages advertising on Facebook Pages that you own or administer, using Meta’s official Graph API. It runs only with access you explicitly grant, and only on the Pages you connect.

01Information we access

With your authorization, and only through Meta’s official APIs, the App may access:

  • Your Facebook Pages and their basic information (name and ID).
  • The ability to create, schedule, and publish posts on those Pages.
  • Public engagement on those Pages — comments and reactions — so it can reply and report.
  • The Page message inbox you connect, to answer common questions.
  • Advertising data for ad accounts you manage, to create and monitor campaigns you authorize.

The App does not access your personal profile content, your friends list, or any data unrelated to the Pages you connect. It does not read the private messages of anyone outside the Page inboxes you authorize.

02How we use it

Accessed data is used only to operate the features you ask for:

  • Publishing and scheduling content you have prepared.
  • Responding to comments and messages on your Pages.
  • Creating and monitoring advertising you have authorized.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiles or resale of any kind.

03Storage & retention

Access tokens and operational data — such as your queue of scheduled posts and their results — are stored locally on the operator’s own device, solely to run the App. Tokens are never shared. Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the App and is removed when you revoke access.

04Deleting your data & revoking access

You can end the App’s access at any time from Facebook Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites). Revoking immediately invalidates the App’s tokens and stops all access. To request deletion of any data the App still holds, contact us at the email below and it will be deleted.

05Third-party services

The App communicates only with Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram) through their official Graph API, and with Anthropic’s API to help generate text content. It does not transmit your Page data to any other third party.

06Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above always reflects the current version; material changes will be reflected here.

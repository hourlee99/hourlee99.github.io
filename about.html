Tech + marketing entrepreneur · Phnom Penh, Cambodia

Most people in tech pick a side. They become a developer who ships clean code but can't get anyone to use it — or a marketer who can sell anything but can't build the thing being sold. I never picked. I learned both, because a business doesn't need a developer or a marketer. It needs results.

So I build the systems businesses run on — POS, online ordering, memberships, AI content machines, growth automation — and I run REÂM, a marketing agency that fills real tables for Cambodia's restaurants and cafés. When De Brazil Steakhouse got $3.40 back for every $1 of ad spend, that wasn't luck. It was the same builder's mindset, pointed at growth.

I work Khmer-first, for Cambodian businesses, because the copy-pasted Western playbook doesn't fit how people here actually choose where to eat and what to buy. And I build in public — you see the work, the numbers, and the thinking, so you can decide for yourself.

About Cyclo & Co.

Footwear for the everyday journey

Cyclo & Co. started in a small Phnom Penh workshop with one idea: shoes that keep up with real life. Named after the city's beloved cyclos, we build footwear meant to move — comfortable enough for the daily commute, refined enough for the room you walk into.

Every pair is finished by hand using ethically sourced leather and materials chosen to last. We keep our range small and considered, so each style earns its place in your rotation. When you buy a pair, we plant a tree along Cambodia's riverbanks.

Crafted to last

Hand-finished construction and full-grain leather that ages beautifully with wear.

Made responsibly

Ethically sourced materials and fair workshops — quality you can feel good about.

Delivered nationwide

Free delivery across Cambodia, with easy 30-day returns on every order.
